# Prompt pack — website / landing page

```
Build a landing page for <SUBJECT> — <one line on audience and job>.

Hero: <the most characteristic thing in the subject's world, as a concrete element —
a product shot, a single number, a sentence, an image — not a gradient blob>.
Sections in order: nav · hero · proof (real only) · bento features · showcase ·
pricing (if applicable) · FAQ · closing CTA · poster footer.

Type: display <Fraunces / Instrument Serif> at 72–120px, tracking −0.03em, 2–3 lines ·
body <Inter Tight> 17px/28 · eyebrow 12px +0.12em, used at most twice per page.
Palette: bg <hex> · ink <hex> · one accent <hex> used exactly three times on the page.
Spacing: sections 120–200px desktop / 64px mobile · text max-width 720px.
Motion (two only): 1) headline word-stagger 60ms on load, 2) sections fade+rise 24dp at
20% visibility. Everything else static. Reduced motion respected.

Banned: purple/blue gradients, abstract blob art instead of the product, "trusted by"
logos the product does not have, stock photos of people pointing, 3-column pricing with a
scaled middle card, cookie-cutter "Welcome to…" copy.
```

## Copy pass
```
Rewrite the copy: sentence case, active voice, one job per line. CTA names the outcome
("Start a design", not "Get started"). No superlatives, no "seamlessly", no "unlock".
Every claim must be checkable from the brief.
```
