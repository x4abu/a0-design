# FontInLogo — what typeface famous brands actually use

**What it is.** A searchable database of the typefaces behind world brands, film/TV/game
titles, plus the closest free alternatives. Useful because it converts "premium" into a
concrete font name.

## Patterns from the database worth knowing
- **Brand wordmarks are almost never default UI fonts.** Adidas (ITC Avant Garde,
  modified), Airbnb (Cereal), Alphabet (Product Sans), Adobe (Myriad / Adobe Clean),
  Amazon (ITC Officina Sans), BBC (Gill Sans), Bershka (Trade Gothic), Aston Martin
  (Optima). All custom or licensed — which is exactly why they read as expensive.
- **Display = personality, body = invisibility.** Every one of those brands sets body copy
  in something neutral and puts the character in the wordmark and headlines.
- **Title cards** lean on free fonts that carry a strong voice: Anton (Thunderbolts),
  Bebas Neue (Netflix), Cinzel (Game of Thrones), Orbitron (Star Wars/Stranger Things),
  Black Ops One (Jurassic Park). Great for a poster, wrong for a product UI.

## What this means for a0
Pick one **display** face with real character (Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Playfair Display,
or a licensed grotesk like Satoshi / General Sans) and one **neutral UI** face (Inter Tight,
Manrope, Figtree). Set the display at a large size with tight tracking; that single pairing
does most of the "premium" work. Never let the UI font be the only voice on a hero.

**Free alternatives that punch above their weight:** Instrument Serif, Fraunces, Playfair
Display, Bricolage Grotesque, Archivo Black, Space Grotesk, Manrope, Inter Tight,
Schibsted Grotesk, Geist, Geist Mono.
