# Mobbin — evidence from shipped products

**What it is.** A searchable library of real mobile apps, web apps and websites:
individual screens, end-to-end flows (onboarding, checkout, account setup, subscription),
UI patterns (dialogs, navigation, forms, tables, empty states, paywalls) and website
sections (heroes, pricing, testimonials, nav, footers).

**How to use it (their own guidance, and it is right).** Treat it as *evidence of how real
products are designed*, not proof that a pattern improved performance. Compare multiple
examples, keep the source link, separate observation from inference, then adapt to your
brand and constraints.

## Flows worth copying structurally
- **Onboarding** — 3–5 steps max, one decision per screen, progress dots not percentages,
  skip always visible, the first screen sells the outcome not the feature.
- **Account setup** — ask for the minimum, defer everything else to first use; show what
  you get before the permission prompt.
- **Checkout** — order summary pinned, one CTA per step, inline validation, saved payment
  method first.
- **Paywall** — plan comparison in ≤3 rows, the recommended plan marked once (not scaled
  by 1.05 with a "Most popular" sticker on all three), restore-purchase link always.
- **Empty state** — illustration or mascot, one sentence of what belongs here, one action.

## Pattern conventions that read as "premium"
Large titles that collapse into a compact nav bar on scroll; segmented controls for
mutually exclusive views; bottom sheets for anything that does not deserve a full screen;
skeletons that match the final layout exactly; destructive actions in red text behind a
sheet, never a primary button.
