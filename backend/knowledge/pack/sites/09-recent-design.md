# Recent.design — what shipped this week

**What it is.** A fast-moving inspiration feed across web, interface, branding, motion,
illustration, posters and print. Also lists design jobs, which is a useful signal of which
studios are setting the current bar.

**How to use it.** Set a cadence: once a week, open it, pick three pieces, and write down
*one transferable decision* from each (not "looks nice"). Examples of a usable note:
"nav is a single pill with 4 items, active item is a filled black capsule"; "headline is
serif, body is grotesk, no third font"; "the only colour is the accent in the CTA and one
chart series".

**Signal to noise.** The feed mixes real product work with poster art. Filter for
interface/motion; ignore anything you cannot express as tokens, spacing or motion.
