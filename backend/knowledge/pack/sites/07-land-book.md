# Land-book — 10,000+ curated websites, filterable

**What it is.** A daily-updated gallery of hand-picked websites, with filters that double
as a taxonomy of modern web design: style (3D, Apple-style, big type, brutalism, cards,
dark, flat, gradient, light, parallax, pastel, stripe, visible borders), type (landing,
portfolio, ecommerce, SaaS, mobile app, single page), typography (display sans / serif /
serif), colour, and platform (Webflow, Framer, Shopify, WordPress).

**What to take.** The filter vocabulary is the lesson: the difference between a site that
looks designed and one that looks generated is almost always **big type, one bold layout
idea, and a visible grid**. Their "Big Type" and "Big Type in Footer" tags are the single
most-copied premium move of the last two years.

## Patterns worth stealing
- **Oversized headline that breaks the grid** — 96–160px display type, tight tracking,
  set in 2–3 lines, with the last line indented or overlapping an image.
- **Footer as a poster** — the brand name at 20vw, cropped, with the legal links tiny
  underneath. Costs nothing, reads expensive.
- **Editorial rhythm** — alternating full-bleed image and narrow text column; 120–200px
  section padding; max text width 720px.
- **Hairline system** — 1px rules between sections instead of cards, with generous air.
- **One accent, huge restraint** — the best examples use a near-monochrome palette plus
  one warm accent used exactly three times on the page.

## What to avoid
Gradient blobs, "trusted by" strips of logos the product is not associated with, stock
photos of people pointing at laptops, 3-column pricing with a scaled middle card.
