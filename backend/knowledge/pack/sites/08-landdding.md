# Landdding — fresh landing pages and templates

**What it is.** A curated gallery of new website designs and templates across Webflow,
Framer, WordPress, Wix and Squarespace (~2.7k designs, ~1.9k designers, 40 categories).
Skews toward studio, portfolio, fintech and product-launch work.

**What to take.** It is the best place to see *current* structure rather than 2021-era
SaaS layouts. Study how the first screen is composed:
- a single sentence of positioning in the top-left, a real product shot to the right;
- nav that is either a single line of text links or a floating pill, never a mega-menu;
- one proof element (a number, a logo row of *actual* customers, or a single testimonial);
- the CTA repeated exactly twice on the page, with identical wording.

## Reusable structures
1. **Studio grid** — 12-column grid, work tiles of 3 sizes, hover reveals a caption.
2. **Launch page** — hero with a countdown-free "available now" line, three feature
   blocks in a bento, a changelog strip, email capture.
3. **Fintech product** — dark or cream canvas, one number as the hero (the rate, the fee),
   a phone mockup showing the actual flow, compliance line in 12px muted.
4. **Portfolio** — name at display size, three lines of bio, a list of work with years
   aligned right, one email link.
